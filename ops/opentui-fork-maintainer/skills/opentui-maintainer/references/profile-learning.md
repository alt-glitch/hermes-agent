# Profile-local maintainer learning

This is the one profile-owned reference preserved byte-for-byte when
`configure.py` refreshes the versioned `opentui-maintainer` skill. Record only
short, evidence-backed lessons that have not yet been reviewed into
`failure-learning.md`. Keep exact logs in their run directory; do not copy
credentials, private conversations, full transcripts or large incident dumps
here.

Once a lesson is incorporated into versioned guidance, remove its local copy
during a reviewed, quiescent deployment.
